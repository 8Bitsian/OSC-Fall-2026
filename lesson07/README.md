### ℹ️ Introduction
> Some companies used a diverse range of operating systems (OSs) other than Windows, like Linux and macOS.
> Your role involves ensuring seamless operation, security, and support for these systems, enabling end users to focus on their work without technical interruptions.

### #️⃣ Certification Preparation
