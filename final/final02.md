# 2️⃣ Security Breach Research and Presentation
> This project is designed to help you understand the real-world implications of account security breaches, identifying vulnerabilities in security systems, and exploring preventative measures by researching these areas and creating a presentation. By completing this activity, you will gain practical knowledge of cybersecurity concepts, risk mitigation strategies, and the importance of proactive security measures in safeguarding information.
>
> This exercise supports the understanding of the following objective for the A+ Core 2 (220-1202) Exam:
> - 2.4 Summarize types of malware and tools/methods for detection, removal, and prevention
>
> By completing this project, you will:
> - Identify how and why security breaches occur
> - Analyze cybersecurity incidents and identify vulnerabilities
> - Determine effective strategies for preventing security breaches
> - Explore the importance of continuous learning and adaptation in the field of cybersecurity
> 
> For this project, you will:
> 
> **1. Research a recent account security breach (within the last 2-3 years) that has been widely reported in the news or cybersecurity industry and gathered detailed information about the breach:**
> - The organization or platform affected
> - The timeline of events (e.g., when the breach occurred, when it was discovered, etc.)
> - The scope of the breach (e.g., number of accounts compromised, type of data exposed, etc.)
> - The root cause(s) of the breach and how the attackers exploited these weaknesses. Consider factors such as:
>   - Technical vulnerabilities (e.g., weak passwords, unpatched software, misconfigured systems, etc.
>   - Human errors (e.g., phishing attacks, insider threats, lack of training, etc.
>   - Systemic issues (e.g., lack of monitoring, inadequate security policies, etc.)
> 
> **2. Create a well-designed and accurate audio/visual presentation (5-7 minutes) that include the following components:**
> - Overview of the Breach: Provide a concise summary of the incident
> - Detailed Analysis: Explain what went wrong and identify the key factors that contributed to the breach
> - Prevention Strategies: Based on your research, suggest specific actions that could have prevented the breach. Include:
>   - Technical solutions (e.g., multi-factor authentication, encryption, regular updates, etc.)
>   - Organizational strategies (e.g., employee training, incident response planning, regular audits, etc.)
> - Policy improvements (e.g., stricter access control, compliance with security standards, etc.)
> - Lessons Learned: Discuss the significance of the lessons learned from the breach
> - Broader Implications: Reflect on the impact of the breach on the organization, its customers, and the cybersecurity industry as a whole
> - Key Takeaways: Highlight actionable insights that can help prevent similar incidents in the future
> - Visuals: Use slides, charts, or diagrams to enhance clarity and engagement
>   
> **3. Write a paper (1 to 2 pages) that summarizes your presentation and lessons learned. Include any infographics, charts, etc. you created for your presentation**
