### 5️⃣ Hands-On AI Experimentation
> This project is designed to help you gain practical experience with using AI tools and platforms by exploring an AI tool or platform, documenting your findings, and reflecting on your experience. By completing this activity, you will develop a foundational understanding of how AI technologies work, their potential applications, and how to interact with them effectively.
>
> This exercise supports the understanding of the following objective for the A+ Core 2 (220-1202) Exam:
> - 4.10 Explain basic concepts related to artificial intelligence (AI)
>
> By completing this project, you will:
> - Use an AI tool and platform
> - Identify the basic principles of AI, such as training models, interacting with AI systems, and analyzing outputs
> - Develop problem-solving and critical thinking skills by overcoming challenges during experimentation
> - Identify the potential applications of AI in real-world scenarios
> 
> For this project, you will:
> 
> **1. Select one of the following AI tools or platforms:**
> - Google Teachable Machine: You can use Google Teachable Machine to create and train a machine learning model using images, sounds, or poses
> - IBM Watson: Try IBM Watson to explore AI services such as natural language processing, visual recognition, or chatbot creation
> - OpenAI's ChatGPT: Interact with OpenAI's ChatGPT to analyze the capabilities of a conversational AI model
> 
> **2. For your selected AI tool or platform, complete the following steps in order:**
> - Access the selected AI and create an account if necessary. All the tools offer a free or trial version
> - Follow the tool's tutorial or documentation to understand its features and functionality
> - Create a small project or experiment using the tool.
> 
> _For example, you can train a model to recognize objects or sounds using the Google Teachable Machine, build a simple chatbot using IBM Watson, or use ChatGPT to generate creative text outputs or solve problems._
>   
> **3. Write a paper (1 to 2 pages) documenting the process you used for your project or experiment. Make sure to include the tool you selected, the steps you followed, the challenges you faced, how you overcame them, and the outcome of your experiment or project. Also, make sure to include files or screenshots of your project.**
> 
> **4. Create a short side presentation with audio or a short video to explain how to use the tool selected - a demo of the tool for those who are not familiar. Also, add your reflection about:**
> - What did you learn about the capabilities and limitations of the AI tool you used?
> - How could the AI tool you experimented with be applied in a real-world context?
> - What challenges did you face, and how did you address them?
> - How did this exercise change your perception of AI technologies?
