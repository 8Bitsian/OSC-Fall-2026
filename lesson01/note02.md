# ⏹️ Professional Communication
Keeping a professional conduct by maintaining a positive and helpful attitude while assisting the customer. Understanding and using professional communication in all aspects of the job, on site and remotely, will be critical to your success as an IT specialist.

## *️⃣ Learning Objectives:
As you study this lesson, answer the following:
- What are the characteristics of professional communication?
- How does maintaining a positive attitude affect a service call?
- How should you deal with a difficult situation such as an angry client or customer?
