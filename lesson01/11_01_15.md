# Lab: Close a Ticket
Today is your first day at your new IT help-desk job.
Your boss has assigned you a ticket in Issue Trax, your company's ticketing system.
To complete this lab, search for and open Issue Trax on the **ITAdmin** computer, then read and follow the instructions in the ticket.

## Directions
In this lab your task is to:
- Open the company's help desk ticketing system, **Issue Trax**
- For the ticket assigned to you, add a comment and close the ticket
- Verify that the closed ticket is showed under the **Closed** category of the ticketing system

## Explanation
Complete this lab as follows:
1. From **ITAdmin**, open the company's help desk ticketing system, **Issue Trax**:
  - From the taskbar, select **Start**
  - Select **All apps**
  - Select **Issue Trax**
2. Read description at the top of the ticket **Ticket Training**
3. Close the ticket:
  - In the **New Comment** field, enter the comment
  - Select the **plus sign (+)** to add the comment
  - From the top of the ticket:
    - Select **Save**
    - Select the **left arrow (<-)**
4. Verify that the ticket is closed:
  - From the left pane, select **Closed**
  - Verify that **Ticket Training** is shown
